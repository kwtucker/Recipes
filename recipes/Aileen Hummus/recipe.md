# Hummus

Description goes here

- Time: 10 minutes
- Source: [Inspired Taste](https://www.inspiredtaste.net/15938/easy-and-smooth-hummus-recipe/)

## Grocery

- Chickpeas | 1 15 ounce can |
- Tahini | 1/4 cup |
- Olive oil | 2 table spoons
- Lemon juice | 1 lemon (60 ml)|
- Garlic | 1 
- Water | 2 or 3 table spoons
- Cumin | 1/2 teaspoon
- Salt
- Sesame Oil
- Paprika (topping)

1. Whip the tahini and lemon juice in food processor for 1 minute. Color changes to a lighter tone
2. Scrape bowl, whip for 30 seconds more
3. Add olive oil, minced garlic, cumin, and 1/2 teaspoon of salt to the tahini and lemon juice. Process for 30 seconds.
4. Scrape the bowl, and whip for 30 more seconds.
5. Add half of the chickpeas, process for 1 minute
6. Scrape bowl and add the remaining of chickpeas, process for 1 to 2 minutes.
7. The hummus will be thick, slowly add 2 to 3 tablespoons of water
8. Taste and adjust with more salt or more lemon
9. Serve with drizzle of olive oil and sprinkle of paprika.
